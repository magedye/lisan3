## 2. Root-level corrective decisions

### 2.1 Anf — ا ن ف

- **Previous proposed nucleus:** a foremost or leading front-extremity, with
  the nose as the face's projecting front and `ءَانِفًا` as the leading edge of
  just-elapsed time.
- **Why it failed:** `missing internal Quranic bridge`; `metaphorical bridge
  insufficiently evidenced`; `verifier objection not genuinely reconciled`;
  `insufficient occurrence evidence`; `unresolved semantic ambiguity`.
- **Corrected analysis:** the two 5:45 tokens establish an anatomical
  nose-class. The single 47:16 token establishes a temporal-adverbial class.
  Nothing in the three Quranic occurrences demonstrates the spatial-to-
  temporal transition. The adversarial verifier's bridge relies explicitly on
  later lexical examples, so it cannot cure the internal evidentiary gap.
- **Usage classes:** (A) anatomical `ٱلْأَنف`, 2 occurrences; (B) temporal
  `ءَانِفًا`, 1 occurrence. These are a provisional partition, not a claim of
  historical homonymy.
- **Semantic boundaries:** do not infer temporal recency from anatomical
  projection; do not infer a projecting tip from 47:16; do not use `Awl`,
  `qdm`, or later lexical derivatives as the missing bridge.
- **Final epistemic result:** `UNRESOLVED`.
- **Synthesis-ready:** **No**. Both observed classes may be represented, but
  no shared root nucleus may be synthesized.

### 2.2 qwm — ق و م

- **Previous proposed nucleus:** rising into and holding an erect, upright
  standing, extended through establishment, maintenance, rectitude,
  subsistence, stations, Resurrection, and `qawm` as a body standing together.
- **Why it failed:** `multiple genuine usage classes`; `over-broad proposed
  nucleus`; `derivational specialization incorrectly collapsed`; `verifier
  objection not genuinely reconciled`.
- **Corrected analysis:** 163 non-plain-`N` occurrences form an internally
  connected productive complex of physical standing/rising, causing or
  maintaining establishment, stable continuance, rectitude/straightness, and
  standing-place/event. Those facets must remain visible rather than be
  reduced to literal vertical posture. The 497 plain-`N` `qawm` occurrences
  form a communal-human collective class. Their co-text does not activate
  standing, maintenance, or rectitude; some explicitly describe the people as
  fallen, blind, or astray. “Standing together” is therefore circular and
  non-discriminating.
- **Usage classes:** (A) productive standing/establishment/maintenance/
  rectitude complex, 163 occurrences with derivational subfacets; (B) `qawm`
  communal collective, 497 occurrences.
- **Semantic boundaries:** physical erection is not invariant in rectitude or
  maintenance; communal identity is not an instance of erection; `qawm` is not
  distinguished from other collective nouns by an unexpressed standing image.
- **Cross-root differentiation:** the `Ass / bny / qwm` relation applies only
  to the establishment branch: `Ass` supplies a base, `bny` an erected
  construction, and productive `qwm` standing/establishment. It does not
  license the `qawm` collective. The `qawm ... ḍāllīn` co-text also prevents
  extending the `mustaqīm` pole to the communal noun.
- **Final epistemic result:** `MULTIPLE_IRREDUCIBLE_QURANIC_USAGE_CLASSES`.
- **Synthesis-ready:** **Yes, as a two-class partition only**; no root-wide
  universal nucleus.

  dependents are not the head; possession is not priority; outcome is not

### 2.4 Sdr — ص د ر

- **Why it failed:** `multiple genuine usage classes`; `over-broad proposed

  objection not genuinely reconciled`.
- **Corrected analysis:** all 44 noun occurrences profile the breast/chest as
  an enclosing interior or capacity-seat for concealed, affective, or
  cognitive content. `22:46` sharply distinguishes hearts located **inside**
  breasts. The two verbal occurrences profile proceeding or causing a group

  live component of the noun class, and retention cannot count as proof of
  release.

  44; (B) verbal proceeding/causing-to-proceed from a source, 2.
- **Semantic boundaries:** chest is not `qalb`; interiority is not frontality;
  concealment, constriction, and expansion do not imply egress; external
  removal of content does not make the chest a source of emission.
- **Cross-root differentiation:** verbal `Sdr` profiles departure from a
  source-side situation; `xrj` profiles exit across an inside/outside boundary.
  That local contrast does not bridge the noun and verb classes of `Sdr`.
- **Final epistemic result:** `MULTIPLE_IRREDUCIBLE_QURANIC_USAGE_CLASSES`.
- **Synthesis-ready:** **Yes, as two separate classes only**.

### 2.5 rbb — ر ب ب

- **Previous proposed nucleus:** custodial mastership as a single fused
  owner–sustainer–governor–rearer relation, with a reciprocal reared pole.
- **Why it failed:** `over-broad proposed nucleus`; `derivational
  specialization incorrectly collapsed`; `verifier objection not genuinely
  reconciled`; `insufficient occurrence evidence`; `unresolved semantic
  ambiguity`.
- **Corrected analysis:** the large `rabb/arbāb` family supports an
  asymmetrical relation of authoritative charge over a dependent domain; the
  co-text may realize ownership, governing, provision, or sustaining without
  requiring all facets simultaneously. `rabāʾib` at 4:23 names the dependent
  party reared in care. The three `rabbāniyy` occurrences have co-textual
  teaching, guarding, judging, or restraint roles, but the packet cannot decide
  whether the derivation contributes affiliation to the Rabb, custodial
  function, or both. `ribbiyyūn` at 3:146 is internally opaque beyond a plural
  group fighting with a prophet. External derivational alternatives are not
  used either to reconcile or refute it.
- **Usage classes:** (A) authoritative charge-holder (`rabb/arbāb`); (B)
  reared dependent (`rabāʾib`); (C) derived religious-role/affiliation
  (`rabbāniyy`), resistant; (D) `ribbiyyūn`, unresolved.
- **Semantic boundaries:** lordship is not identical to worship-worthiness;
  charge does not require a four-facet fusion; passive dependence and
  affiliation cannot be silently reversed into mastery; local role co-text is
  not automatically the root's lexical contribution.
- **Final epistemic result:** `RESISTANT_OCCURRENCES_REMAIN`.
- **Synthesis-ready:** **No**. The master-relation class is usable, but the
  root-wide result is not ready while four derived-form tokens remain

- `rjm`: **directed casting or projection toward a target**, qualified by

  communal `qawm` without evidence.
- `rbb` class-local center: **an asymmetrical authoritative-charge relation**,
  without requiring ownership, sustenance, government, and rearing to be
  simultaneously active.
- `ETw` class-local center: **directed conferment/allocation from source to
  recipient**, without hand, discrete-object, or pre-existence requirements.
- `fqr` class-local center: **insufficiency, need, or dependency**, internally
  delimited by `ghny`; the 75:25 event is excluded pending evidence.

  (`ETw`);

## 6. New differentiation axes

1. **Productive derivation vs lexicalized class:** productive `qwm` versus
   communal `qawm`; productive `SlH` versus proper-name `Ṣāliḥ`.
2. **Head vs member vs bearer:** order/priority, household affiliation, and
   possession in `Awl` are different relational roles.
3. **Container vs motion-from-source:** breast interiority and verbal
   proceeding in `Sdr` must not validate each other automatically.
