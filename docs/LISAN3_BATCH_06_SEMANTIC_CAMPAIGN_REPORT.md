## Major semantic boundaries

- Root contribution remains separate from form, construction, participant, and
  context. For example, `qtl` supplies lethal opposition while reciprocity
  comes from Form III/VIII; `fSl` supplies distinction while judicial authority
  comes from context.
- Moral polarity is not placed inside roots when participant/object evidence
  accounts for it. This is material for `$rk`, `mkr`, `swA`, `Hlf`, `wSf`, and
  `$hw`.
- Referential certainty is not inflated into abstract certainty. `ArD`, `Hdq`,
  `Eyr`, `Ezr`, `HDD`, `Hfw`, and `Hwj` remain MODERATE because their form or
  context diversity is narrow.
- Dominant-frequency success never overrides a resistant token. `jwb`, `Sdd`,
  `ysr`, `srr`, `fwq`, `HrD`, and `Sbg` remain WEAK despite coherent major
  families.
- Proper names do not self-authorize etymological decomposition. `SbA` remains
  UNRESOLVED even though its group reference is certain.

## Candidate cross-root relations

- `rsl` directed dispatch complements `H$r` convergence into assembly without
  making sending and gathering synonymous.
- `qtl` supplies lethal action/contest; `HDD` and `HrD` supply urging/incitement.
  Motivation toward combat is not combat itself.
- `$rk` introduces shared participation, while `ESm` maintains a restraining
  bond. Joining and preventing separation are distinct relational structures.
- `srr` withholds content, while `mkr` covertly arranges an outcome. Secrecy may
  serve a plot but is not its strategic structure.
- `qlb` reorients inner state; `Dyq` constricts available capacity; `Hzn` burdens
  the self with sorrow. These can co-occur without semantic collapse.
- `$hw` profiles appetitive pull, whereas `Hwj` profiles need/purpose; desire is
  not always need, and practical need is not always appetite.
- `fwq` marks upper position/rank, `Sgr` scalar lowness, and `kvr` abundance.
  Their scalar behavior is related but their dimensions remain distinct.

These relations are hypotheses for later review only. No graph edge or
canonical cross-root relation was created.
