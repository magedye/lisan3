while the host prevents false source/evidence/tool/coverage claims and keeps

## AI research authority

AI may observe, induce, generate alternatives, compare, seek counterevidence,
falsify, reject, prefer, explain, and persist a Research Judgment for a root,
lexeme, local occurrence, verse, or semantic distinction. `UNRESOLVED` is a
valid result.

AI may not set Canonical Acceptance, change the method/source/terminology, or
self-certify evidence existence, tool execution, or coverage.

## Host enforcement

The backend, not prompt obedience, enforces:

1. production-valid corpus and exact method revision;
2. isolated context and real tool allowlist;
3. resolvable same-run evidence references;
4. claim-sensitive coverage derived from stored evidence;
5. falsification for preferred important results;
6. explicit independent verification and server-owned canonicalization;
7. audit/provenance and reopening on material new evidence.

Methodological checks that do not prove a hard-boundary violation are
diagnostics, not gates. There is no research Lock or Purity gate.

## Semantic method

Preserve the material distinctions:

`root -> derivation/inflection -> lexeme -> form -> construction -> local form/participant roles -> context/discourse -> local meaning -> final statement`

Do not assign contextual, morphological, or constructional contribution to the
root. Use the Root Concept definition and answer templates in the active
semantic skill. Do not force unity or a distinctive difference.

## Minimal workflow

1. Create a run with target contract and expression; host resolves authority.
2. Build isolated context and record observations/hypotheses as useful. Their
   order is methodological, not an administrative state machine.
3. Challenge material alternatives and falsify an important preferred result.
4. Persist `PREFERRED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `REJECTED` Research Judgment through
   deterministic validators.
5. Only when official project knowledge is requested: independently verify the
   current revision, then explicitly canonicalize it.

Accepted root memory is retrieved automatically for later questions, with its
origin and state. It is project knowledge, not Quranic evidence. New material
evidence reopens it without overwriting history.
